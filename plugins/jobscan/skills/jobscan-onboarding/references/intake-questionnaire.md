# Intake questionnaire

Ask conversationally, a few at a time. Capture real numbers. Flag any missing metric and ask a follow-up
rather than inventing one. Examples are field-neutral — adapt them to the user's discipline.

Plain-language version for prospective users: `docs/INTERVIEW-QUESTIONS.md` — edit both when questions change.

## A. Identity, positioning, constraints
1. Name, city/state (or country), email, phone, LinkedIn, any portfolio/lab site.
2. Current title and employer; career stage (grad student, postdoc, early-career, established pivoting, …).
3. In 1–2 sentences: how do you want to be positioned? What throughline should every application communicate?
4. The failure mode to avoid — what shouldn't applications collapse into (the narrow identity that undersells
   your real value)?
5. Work authorization: citizen/PR, visa status, countries needing sponsorship?
6. Countries/regions you'd relocate to even without authorization, if sponsorship were offered?
7. Location preferences (urban/rural, political lean, regions to prioritize/avoid). Are remote roles exempt?
8. Salary floor (absolute min), preferred/target range, higher floor if relocation required.
9. Government pay-scale floors to respect (min grade/band)?
10. Role types, sectors, or employers to explicitly avoid, and why (your "avoid-list").

## B. Headline accomplishments (numbers)
11. Total grant/funding secured (aggregate) and the single largest award (amount, funder, project, your role).
12. Large projects/programs led — scale (samples, plots, datasets, years, partner organizations).
13. Supervisory/mentoring — how many people, over what period, doing what.
14. Publications: total, **published/DOI'd separated from in-review**. Confirm: never list in-review as
    published? (Recommend yes.)
15. Presentations/outreach — how many, audience sizes, languages, standout engagement numbers.
16. Awards/scholarships/honors — with amounts/rankings.
17. Certifications/licenses relevant to the field.

## C. Skill inventory
18. Technical/lab skills and tools.
19. Data/analysis tools (R, Python, pipelines, stats, GIS) — honest strength vs. ramp-up.
20. Hands-on generative-AI/LLM experience (tools, uses)?
21. Project/program management (budgets, grant admin, team leadership, protocols).
22. Regulatory/compliance (permits, biosafety, import/export, licensing).
23. Communication/teaching/outreach; multilingual ability.
24. "Hidden" transferable skills from non-academic jobs worth reframing.

## D. Roles & experience
25. Each position (reverse chronological): title, employer, location, dates, 3–6 quantified bullets.
26. Earlier/non-academic jobs worth keeping for transferable-skill evidence.

## E. Leadership, service, framing
27. Elected/appointed positions, committees, society involvement.
28. Negotiation/bargaining/advocacy experience.
29. How do you describe your dissertation/postdoc now? How should it read to a non-specialist recruiter?
30. Which field terms need plain-language translation vs. which should stay because target roles expect them?

## F. Track record & voice
31. Past successful applications/interviews/offers — what specifically worked?
32. Real past cover letters or interview transcripts that landed well? (Best raw material for the voice file.)
33. Interview questions you've been asked that reveal what employers screen for?
34. Writing preferences: tone, sentence-length comfort, liked/avoided phrasings, hard "never do this" rules,
    and any minimum font size for documents.

## G. Target roles & search scope
35. Job titles/sectors you're aiming for.
36. Domain keywords for search — and flag any **asymmetric pairs** that must both be searched separately.
37. Geographic scope (states, international regions, remote-only?) and any domestic/international split quota.
38. Adjacent sectors to screen out.

## H. Interview story seeds
39. 3–5 real anecdotes (a success, a failure/lesson, a conflict resolved, a mentoring moment, explaining
    something technical to a non-expert) → STAR answers.

## I. Tooling & environment
40. Runtime (Claude Code CLI, Desktop, claude.ai) — is the Skills feature available?
41. Firecrawl connected (API key)? If not, note the built-in fallback.
42. Browser tool available (for JS portals)?
43. Markdown→.docx path (R `officer`, pandoc, Word, Google Docs)?
44. Archive folder path, and do you want the weekly scheduled version?
